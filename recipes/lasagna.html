Description

lasagna is a classic italian dish made with layers of pasta,meat sauce,and chease.

It is baked in the oven until the cheese becomes golden and delicious.

Ingredients

  • lasagna pasta sheets
  • ground beef
  • tomato sauce
  • mozzarella cheese
  • Ricotta cheese
  • salt and pepper

steps

  1. preheat the oven to 180c
  2. cook the ground beef in a pan
  3. Add the tomato sauce and mix well
  4. prepare the pasta sheets
  5. layer the pasta ,meat sauce,and cheese in a baking dish
  6. bake for 40 min
  7. let it cool slightly,then serve.
