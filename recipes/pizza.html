Description

Pizza is a popular Italian dish made of a round, flat dough base topped with tomato sauce, melted cheese, and various toppings like vegetables, meat, or herbs, then baked in an oven.

Ingredients

  • flour
  • water
  • yeast
  • salt
  • olive oil
  • suger

steps

  1. Mix the ingredients
  2. Knead the dough
  3. Let it rise
  4. Preheat the oven
  5. Roll out the dough
  6. Bake the pizza.
